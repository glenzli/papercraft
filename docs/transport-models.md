## 双节公交 / Articulated bus

前后车身以 24 mm 间隙安装，后车保持同向。两侧各一张风琴折棚：38 mm 高、六段各 6 mm 展开长度，附前后各 9 mm 粘贴安装翼。底部连接带宽 12 mm，六段各 5 mm，附前后各 10 mm 安装翼。三张连接纸片均有对应的交替山折/谷折线和 FRONT / REAR 标记；侧安装翼贴到实际端墙，底部安装翼贴到底板。

Install the two bodies in the same direction with a 24 mm gap. Each side bellows is a single
38 mm high sheet with six 6 mm developed pleats and integral 9 mm end flanges. The underside
strap is 12 mm wide with six 5 mm pleats and 10 mm end flanges. All three sheets include alternating
mountain/valley folds and FRONT / REAR marks. Side flanges contact end walls; strap flanges contact
the body floors.

先压折所有折线，用 24 mm 临时垫块保持间隙，再粘两端安装翼；折棚中段不要涂胶。连接件建议另用 100–120 g/m² 纸，车壳用较厚纸。底带粘好后移除垫块，从左右各 15° 轻转试装；若拉紧或起皱立即减小角度。它是柔性纸接头，没有硬质转轴。

Score all folds before gluing. Keep a temporary 24 mm spacer between bodies, then glue only the
end flanges. Leave pleats unglued. Use 100–120 gsm paper for flexible parts and heavier stock for
bodies. Remove the spacer after the strap sets and begin with gentle 15° trials each way. Reduce
the angle if paper pulls or wrinkles. This is a flexible paper joint without a rigid pivot.

## 验证边界 / Validation limits

自动检查覆盖闭合车壳、面邻接、法线、三角形保留、边长、接缝配对、整面和粘贴舌不重叠、A4 页边距，以及从打印折向反推立体朝向。公交左右 15° 的车身间隙和纸长余量通过几何检查；底带转动时还会扭转，纸厚、胶层、刚度、连接强度和重复转动寿命必须经过实物剪折试装。本项目尚未完成这项实物验收。

Automated checks cover closed shells, adjacency, normals, complete triangle coverage, edge
lengths, seam pairs, whole-face/tab overlap, A4 margins and 3D handedness reconstructed from printed
fold directions. Bus body clearance and developed paper length pass at ±15°. Its underside strap
also twists during turning. Paper thickness, glue layers, stiffness, joint strength and repeated
motion require physical cutting/folding trials, which have not been completed.
