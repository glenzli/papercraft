两厢车与皮卡都有真正的凹轮拱和四个独立八角纸轮。每轮由胎面带、内外两张轮片组成，平底落地，中心距地 10 mm。离地底架最低处为 6 mm，四个一体支臂以 7×7 mm 端面粘对应 FL / FR / RL / RR 内轮片；无旋转轴。轮拱与胎面有几何间隙，皮卡拱顶到顶板保留 4 mm。先试合四轮水平再粘接。货斗真正开放，图案朝内，外侧为白纸背。

Hatchback and pickup have real recessed wheel arches and four separate octagonal wheels. Each
wheel has a tread belt and two closing discs, with a flat ground face and centre 10 mm above ground.
The integral underframe clears the ground by 6 mm; four arms glue to matching FL / FR / RL / RR
inner discs through 7×7 mm pads. Wheels are fixed without axles. Arches clear the tyres and the pickup
retains a 4 mm web above each arch. Dry-fit four-wheel alignment. The open bed prints inside,
with a white paper back outside.

旅行机长桨叶实际横向跨度超过 43 mm，中央 8×8 mm 区贴合 8×8×6 mm 轮毂，轮毂贴真实机头端盖。桨平面与机头相距 6 mm；正俯视仍会看到立式桨叶的窄边，斜视更易观察。翼根一体安装翼为 6 mm，尾鳍安装翼为 5 mm。沿 WING / TAIL / FIN 区安装。

The touring plane's two-blade span exceeds 43 mm, with its 8×8 mm centre glued to an
8×8×6 mm hub on the real nose cap. The blade plane clears the nose by 6 mm. Exact top view
still sees this vertical blade edge-on; an oblique view shows its surface. Wing flanges are 6 mm
and the fin flange is 5 mm. Attach at WING / TAIL / FIN.

客机使用十二面圆筒机身、收尖机头/机尾、带微上反角的后掠翼与翼梢，两个八面发动机通过斜顶吊架贴合翼下。吊架端面为 5×12 mm，位于翼前缘之后。主翼先用同形余纸背面补强，避开折线，再挂发动机。展示底座为 70×70 mm，支柱顶 6×24 mm 贴机腹，明确没有起落架。

The jet uses a twelve-sided barrel, nose/tail cones, swept wings with slight dihedral and winglets.
Two octagonal engines fit below through pylons with sloped tops and 5×12 mm attachment pads,
behind the wing leading edge. Reinforce each main wing with a matching offcut before hanging
engines, leaving folds free. A 70×70 mm base and 6×24 mm post top support the belly; no landing gear.

直升机滑橇平底落地，横梁与四根支柱真实连接机腹；桅杆斜底贴机顶，旋翼座连接主旋翼。长旋翼应背面补强，尾桨有独立安装座，旋翼不可转动。水上飞机沿用升高 17 mm 的旅行机身，通过四根 5×6 mm 斜支撑连接两根实体浮筒，既不是涂装变体，也不防水或保证漂浮。

The helicopter's flat skids, crossbars and four cabin posts physically meet the belly. The sloped
mast meets the roof and supports a fixed rotor hub. Reinforce the long rotor; the tail rotor has
its own mounting block. The seaplane raises the touring airframe by 17 mm and uses four diagonal
5×6 mm struts to connect two real pontoons. It is a distinct geometry, not a repaint, and is not
waterproof or certified to float.

飞机均为固定展示纸模，翼面/旋翼单面印刷、背面白色；建议 200–220 g/m² 纸。涂装包括原创蓝白民航、橙紫与奶油绿客机、救援与森林作业直升机、复古与海岸浮筒机；旅行机有天空、复古、救援三款。组装指南和 PDF 使用同一双语步骤，资源包保留步骤。

All aircraft are fixed display models. Sheets print on one side with white backs; use 200–220 gsm
stock. Original liveries include civilian, sunset and vintage jets; rescue, medical and utility
helicopters; vintage/coastal seaplanes; sky, vintage and rescue touring planes. Guides and PDFs
share bilingual instructions, also retained in model packages.

## 双节公交 / Articulated bus

前后车身以 24 mm 间隙安装，后车保持同向。两侧各一张风琴折棚：38 mm 高、六段各 6 mm 展开长度，附前后各 9 mm 粘贴安装翼。底部连接带宽 12 mm，六段各 5 mm，附前后各 10 mm 安装翼。三张连接纸片均有对应的交替山折/谷折线和 FRONT / REAR 标记；侧安装翼贴到实际端墙，底部安装翼贴到底板。

Install the two bodies in the same direction with a 24 mm gap. Each side bellows is a single
38 mm high sheet with six 6 mm developed pleats and integral 9 mm end flanges. The underside
strap is 12 mm wide with six 5 mm pleats and 10 mm end flanges. All three sheets include alternating
mountain/valley folds and FRONT / REAR marks. Side flanges contact end walls; strap flanges contact
the body floors.

先压折所有折线，用 24 mm 临时垫块保持间隙，再粘两端安装翼；折棚中段不要涂胶。连接件建议另用 100–120 g/m² 纸，车壳用较厚纸。底带粘好后移除垫块，从左右各 15° 轻转试装；若拉紧或起皱立即减小角度。它是柔性纸接头，没有硬质转轴。

Score all folds before gluing. Keep a temporary 24 mm spacer between bodies, then glue only the
end flanges. Leave pleats unglued. Use 100–120 gsm paper for flexible parts and heavier stock for
bodies. Remove the spacer after the strap sets and begin with gentle 15° trials each way. Reduce
the angle if paper pulls or wrinkles. This is a flexible paper joint without a rigid pivot.

## 验证边界 / Validation limits

自动检查覆盖闭合车壳、面邻接、法线、三角形保留、边长、接缝配对、整面和粘贴舌不重叠、A4 页边距，以及从打印折向反推立体朝向。新增车辆及飞机的轮臂、吊架、底座、滑橇和浮筒支撑接触面已采样检查。公交左右 15° 的车身间隙和纸长余量通过几何检查；底带转动时还会扭转，纸厚、胶层、刚度、连接强度和重复转动寿命必须经过实物剪折试装。本项目尚未完成这项实物验收。

Automated checks cover closed shells, adjacency, normals, complete triangle coverage, edge
lengths, seam pairs, whole-face/tab overlap, A4 margins and 3D handedness reconstructed from printed
fold directions. Attachment pads for wheels, pylons, stands, helicopter supports and float struts are sampled against the actual meshes. Bus body clearance and developed paper length pass at ±15°. Its underside strap
also twists during turning. Paper thickness, glue layers, stiffness, joint strength and repeated
motion require physical cutting/folding trials, which have not been completed.
